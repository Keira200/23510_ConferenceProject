About the Conference

The Student Technology and Innovation Conference brings together the brightest minds in our university to explore the latest trends in technology. This year's theme focuses on how innovation shapes the future of students and the digital landscape. The conference runs for three days and features keynotes, workshops and networking sessions.

Keynote Sessions

Learn from industry experts and academic leaders about emerging technologies and their real-world applications.

Hands-on Workshops

Participate in practical sessions covering Web Development, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Mobile Application Development.

Networking

Connect with fellow students, developers, innovators and technology enthusiasts from across the university.

Conference Highlights

Three-Day Conference

Attend for 1, 2 or 3 days. A 10% discount is applied when you register for the full 3-day attendance.

Workshop Track

Choose the Workshop Participant package to join a specialised workshop of your choice for an additional fee of KSh 750.

Meal Options

Lunch is available at KSh 300 per day. You may also choose the No Lunch option at no additional cost.
